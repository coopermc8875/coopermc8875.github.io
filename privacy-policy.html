Privacy Policy — Money (Ai)

Effective date: November 24, 2026

TradingPlaces Las Vegas ("we," "us") operates the Money (Ai) mobile application (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App does and does not collect, in plain language.

What the App is

Money (Ai) is a stock-market and crypto research tool. It publishes tested trading and investing strategies, a daily research card, long-term investing lists, and a Crypto Currency section covering major cryptocurrencies. It is not a brokerage or a crypto exchange, and it does not execute trades or transactions of any kind.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account or are invited as a tester, we receive your name and email address so we can give you access and contact you about the App.
  • Purchase information. The App is sold through the Apple App Store and Google Play. Your payment is processed by Apple or Google — we never see or store your card number. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock the App.
  • Basic technical information. The App may receive standard technical data needed to deliver content to your device (such as device type and app version).

Information we never collect

  • No brokerage or crypto credentials. We never ask for, receive, or store your brokerage, bank, trading-account, crypto exchange, or wallet logins, keys, or seed phrases.
  • No access to your accounts or money. The App cannot view your portfolio or wallet, place trades, buy or sell crypto, or move money or assets.
  • No location tracking. The App does not track your location.
  • No sale of personal information. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

How we use information

Only to operate the App: granting access, delivering the daily content, processing the purchase through the store, and answering your support questions.

Sharing

We share information only with the services required to run the App (the Apple App Store and Google Play for purchase processing), or when required by law.

Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this same address before it takes effect.
